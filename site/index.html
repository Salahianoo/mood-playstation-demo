What it handles

Every edge case the lounge floor throws at it

Billing that survives a dead battery

Time is never counted up in memory. Elapsed time is always derived from stored timestamps (start, pauses, carried segments), so a crash, a force-quit or a power cut mid-session never loses a single fils.

Pause, wait, transfer

Hold a room while friends arrive, pause for a broken controller, move a group mid-game. Each segment keeps its own rate.

Prepaid time & bookings

Prepaid sessions count down and fire a looping alarm with vibration that jumps staff to the room. Reservations lock a room until the guest shows up.

POS wired to inventory

Selling decrements stock, cancelling returns it, and sold-out items disable themselves. Cost price is snapshotted per sale, so margins stay honest.

Checkout in seconds

Discounts in JOD or percent, cash / Visa / CliQ, a change-due calculator for the cashier, and bill numbers that never repeat.

The owner's books, behind a lock

Shift close with the drawer float, staff cash withdrawals, operating expenses, a 48-hour bill archive, a permanent shift log and a monthly stocktake with play hours, cost of goods and net profit. Staff run the floor; only the owner sees the money.

/// Billed time in the current room: zero while waiting,
/// frozen while paused, net of every past pause.
Duration segmentElapsed(DateTime now) {
  if (isWaiting) return Duration.zero;
  final end = isPaused ? pausedAt! : now;
  final raw = end.difference(timerStartedAt!) - pausedDuration;
  return raw.isNegative ? Duration.zero : raw;
}

/// Across every room this session has been moved through.
Duration elapsed(DateTime now) =>
    carriedElapsed + segmentElapsed(now);
  • Timestamps, not counters. The one-second ticker only triggers repaints. It is never the source of truth.
  • Snapshots over references. Price and cost are frozen onto each sale, so a price change never rewrites history.
  • Two layouts, one codebase. Separate design baselines for a landscape tablet (1280×800) and a phone (390×844), so neither is a blown-up copy of the other.
  • Arabic-first. Full right-to-left UI, Arabic month and weekday names, JOD with three decimals.
